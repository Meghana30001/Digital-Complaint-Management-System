Core Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

✍️

Easy Complaint Filing

Submit a complaint in minutes through a guided 4-step form — fill in your details, select a category, attach supporting evidence, and review before submitting. Each complaint gets a unique tracking ID instantly upon submission.

  • Guided multi-step form with validation
  • Drag-and-drop file attachments
  • Instant unique Complaint ID on submit
🔍

Real-time Status Tracking

Track your complaint anytime using your Complaint ID. View the full resolution timeline, officer remarks, and live status updates — no phone calls needed.

🔐

Three-Role Portal

Dedicated dashboards for Citizens, Officers, and Admins. Each role sees exactly what they need — file and track, manage and resolve, or oversee the whole system.

📊

Live Analytics Dashboard

Real-time KPI cards, monthly complaint trend charts, category breakdowns, and department workload bars — giving admins complete operational visibility at a glance.

🔔

Automated Escalation Alerts

Complaints that exceed their SLA deadline are automatically escalated to department heads. Citizens receive notifications at every stage of the resolution process.

🏗️

Smart Department Routing

When a complaint is submitted, the system automatically identifies the right government department based on the selected category and assigns it to the appropriate officer — no manual sorting needed. Officers are notified instantly and can act right away.

Platform Overview

Built for citizens, officers & administrators

DCMS is a structured online platform that connects citizens directly to government departments. Every complaint is recorded, routed, tracked, and resolved through a clear, transparent process — giving all parties full visibility at every step.

  • Citizen Portal — File complaints, track status, submit feedback
  • Officer Portal — Manage assigned complaints, update status, add remarks
  • Admin Dashboard — Oversee all departments, users, and system analytics
  • Auto-Routing Engine — Assigns each complaint to the right department instantly
  • Full Audit Trail — Every status change is timestamped and logged
  • Feedback System — Citizens rate resolutions; feeds officer performance data
